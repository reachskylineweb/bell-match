Our Company Profile

Igniting sparks of inspiration

The Bell Match Company is an admired organization of professionals renowned for exceptional customer service and an impeccable safety and compliance record. As part of the respected Bell Group of companies with interests in engineering, hotels and pyrotechnics we bring unmatched expertise, fresh ideas and strong financials to every endeavour.

What makes us different is our ability to understand the complete needs of customers, distil the essence and deliver creative solutions that are enduring and inspiring. With well-differentiated product offerings and a sustainable manufacturing approach, we offer customers flexibility, stability and absolute peace of mind!

Spread across 5 acres with a built-up area of over 120,000 square feet, our factory in Sivakasi is equipped with state-of-the-art machinery from Arenco, Herring, Roller Simplex and Columbia — built for scale, precision and compliance.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

1997
Founded in Sivakasi
The Bell Match Company was established in the heart of India's matchbox manufacturing capital, Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu — with a vision for premium quality and global reach.
2004
EN 1783 Certification
Achieved European safety standard EN 1783 certification — opening doors to premium export markets across Europe and the UK.
2010
Factory Expansion to 5 Acres
Completed expansion of our manufacturing facility to 5 acres and over 120,000 sq ft of built-up area, adding Arenco and Herring machinery for enhanced throughput.
2015
FSC Chain of Custody
Received FSC Chain of Custody certification, becoming one of the first match manufacturers in Sivakasi to achieve this eco-responsible standard.
2019
25 Export Destinations
Expanded our global reach to over 25 countries across Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Africa, establishing The Bell Match Company as a truly global manufacturer.
2021
Automated Matchbook Line Launch
Commissioned specialized automated printing and assembly lines for our custom booklet matches and luxury cigar matchboxes, expanding our capacity by 40%.
