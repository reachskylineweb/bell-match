Corporate Policies

Establishing operations rooted in ethical accountability, safety excellence, and continuous environmental and social improvement.

Social Accountability Policy

The Bell Match Company is strictly committed to maintaining a humane, safe, and fair working environment. We strive to align our workplace conditions with respected international guidelines, including the SA 8000 and BSCI frameworks.

  • Child Labor Prohibition: Strict zero-tolerance policy against child labor. We verify age verification documents for all workers and provide protection for young apprentices.
  • Forced Labor: All employees work voluntarily. We strictly prohibit bonded, forced, or compulsory labor practices.
  • Fair Remuneration: Wages paid to workers meet or exceed the legal minimum wage standards, providing basic livelihood and fair allowances.
  • Decent Working Hours: Standard working hours and overtime operate in full compliance with national labor laws and collective bargaining agreements.
  • Non-Discrimination: We provide equal opportunity in hiring, compensation, promotion, and training, free from discrimination based on gender, race, religion, or background.

Environmental Conservation Policy

We believe in sustainable industrial production. As a match manufacturer relying on wood and chemicals, we proactively minimize our ecological footprint at every stage of sourcing and processing.

  • Sustainable Sourcing: By purchasing and processing FSC-certified timber, we support responsible global forestry practices.
  • Waste Management: Wood splint wastage is recycled and reprocessed for auxiliary energy generation inside our steam boilers, achieving near-zero biomass waste.
  • Eco-Friendly Chemical Formulation: All match heads are formulated without harmful sulfur or heavy metals, ensuring zero toxic emissions when struck.

Quality & Safety Policy

Our matches are safe matches. We maintain rigorous testing parameters to protect consumers and industrial handlers.

  • Zero Flying Sparks: Chemical head formulations are balanced to ignite smoothly without spitting fire or emitting hot sparks.
  • Decisive Afterglow Control: All splints are chemically treated to ensure that the flame goes out immediately upon blowing, with zero residual glow.
  • High Splint Strength: Solid aspen wood selection prevents matches from breaking during aggressive strikes on matchbook surfaces.
