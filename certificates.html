Certifications & Compliance

We maintain an impeccable quality and safety compliance record. Our manufacturing processes conform to leading international sustainability, hygiene, and safety protocols.

FSC® Chain of Custody

FSC-C163539

Our voluntarily undertaken Forest Stewardship Council Chain of Custody certification guarantees that the wood used to manufacture our safety matches is sourced strictly from responsibly managed and verified forests.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management

Our operations follow strict, audited quality management guidelines, ensuring high reliability, defect prevention, and batch traceability from processing wood splints to chemical head dipping.

BRCGS Certification

Global Safety Standard

Our manufacturing and processing units operate under BRCGS-certified guidelines to ensure clean, safe, and hygienic matchbox packaging and wood-splint processing, maintaining absolute hygiene and full compliance for domestic and international export demands.

EN 1783 Standard

Safety Matches Compliance

All safety matches are designed, tested, and audited in compliance with European Standard EN 1783. This assures perfect strike sensitivity, zero afterglow, zero flying sparks, and sturdy carton friction surfaces.
