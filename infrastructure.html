How We Make It

Manufacturing Process

Log Yard & Veneer

FSC-certified timber logs are processed in our log yard. Rotary lathes peel thin veneer sheets for splint production with precise thickness control.

Splint Cutting

High-speed Arenco and Herring machines cut veneer sheets into precise matchstick splints of uniform length, width and grain orientation.

Chemical Dipping

Splints are dipped in specially formulated chemical compounds to form the ignition head — paraffin impregnation prevents afterglow.

Boxing & Packaging

Roller Simplex box-filling machines sort, count and fill sleeves. Labels are applied and finished boxes pass final quality inspection before dispatch.
